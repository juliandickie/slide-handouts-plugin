# Changelog

## 0.1.0 - unreleased

First release.

- Reads a PowerPoint or Keynote handout PDF, a deck saved one slide per page from PowerPoint, Keynote or Google Slides, or a PowerPoint or Keynote file converted to PDF.
- Finds every slide and its number with no typed-in positions, and stops with a plain sentence rather than guess when it cannot.
- Keeps each hidden slide's real number when given the original PowerPoint file.
- Builds a handout on A4 or US Letter, portrait or landscape, with 1, 2, 3, 4 or 6 slides per page.
- Notes beside, under, or beside and under each slide, as ruled lines, a dot grid, blank space or a typeable box.
- Line and dot spacing from 4 to 12 mm, and smaller, medium, larger or exact slide widths.
- Places slides as vectors, so they stay sharp.
- Checks every handout before reporting it, and warns about photos that may print soft and slides under 70 mm wide.
- Saves named layouts and a usual layout on the person's own computer.
- A gallery of 11 numbered examples, each usable by name.
- Automatic conversion of PowerPoint and Keynote files, only when asked.
- An export guide for PowerPoint, Keynote and Google Slides.
